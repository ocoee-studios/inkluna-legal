Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Your dreams are private. Inkluna is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not receive your journal content or sleep data on our servers. Store purchases and any exports you choose are handled as described below.

1. Information We Store

Data stored on your device only: Dream journal entries, voice notes, mood data, settings, themes, ink colors, fonts, passcode, and all personal preferences are stored locally on your device using local app storage. Journal entries are not uploaded to Ocoee Studios. Exports and any device-level backup services you enable are separate from Inkluna's local storage.

Sleep data (optional): If you choose to connect a sleep data source, Inkluna reads sleep stage information on your device. On iOS, this uses Apple HealthKit to read sleep stages (Awake, REM, Core, Deep) from Apple Health. On Android, this uses Health Connect to read sleep session data. In both cases, sleep data is read-only, stays on your device, and is never transmitted to any server. Inkluna does not write sleep data. Sleep stages are estimates provided by your device or wearable and are displayed as such.

Data we do NOT collect: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or any personal identifiers. We do not use tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or analytics frameworks that profile users. Inkluna does not upload journal or sleep data to Ocoee Studios or an analytics service. Store purchase processing and exports you choose may communicate with the services described below.

2. Biometric Data

Inkluna supports biometric authentication (Face ID and Touch ID on iOS; fingerprint and biometric on Android) for app lock. Biometric data is handled entirely by your device's operating system. We never access, store, or transmit biometric data. Authentication results are a simple yes/no — we never see your face or fingerprint.

3. How We Use Information

Journal analysis and pattern calculations happen locally on your device. Inkluna uses your dream entries to display your journal, calculate patterns, populate your Dream Book, and show statistics. We do not use your dream content to train models, build user profiles, serve advertisements, or share with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

All journal data is stored locally on your device. On iOS, your passcode is stored in the Keychain via secure encrypted storage. On Android, app-lock credentials are stored using encrypted storage backed by Android Keystore. App lock restricts access through Inkluna's interface; it does not mean that the entire journal database or exported files are encrypted. We recommend keeping your device updated and using a strong device passcode for maximum security.

5. Purchases

Inkluna offers a one-time lifetime purchase to unlock premium features. On iOS, payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. On Android, payments are processed by Google through Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information, credit card numbers, or billing details. The purchase library exchanges purchase information with the relevant store, and Inkluna checks purchase records to determine whether premium features are unlocked. Inkluna does not send purchase records to an Ocoee Studios purchase-validation server. The respective platform's privacy policy governs payment processing.

6. Third-Party Services

On iOS:

Apple StoreKit: Handles in-app purchase processing according to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple HealthKit: Provides read-only access to sleep data with your permission. Data stays on your device.

Apple LocalAuthentication: Handles Face ID / Touch ID for app lock. Biometric data stays on your device.

On Android:

Google Play Billing: Handles in-app purchase processing according to Google's Privacy Policy. Inkluna checks purchase records returned by Google Play to determine access. Ocoee Studios does not receive credit card details through the app.

Health Connect: Provides read-only access to sleep session data with your permission. Data stays on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Android Biometric / Keystore: Handles fingerprint and biometric authentication for app lock. Biometric data stays on your device. App-lock credentials use encrypted storage backed by Android Keystore.

Inkluna does not use advertising or analytics services. The platform services listed above support purchases, optional sleep-data access, and app lock.

7. Notifications

Inkluna can send you a morning reminder to record your dreams. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. On iOS, notifications use the local notification system. On Android, notifications use local alarm scheduling. No notification data is sent to any server, and no push notification tokens are requested or transmitted.

8. Data Export

You can export your dream journal at any time. Exported data is generated on your device and shared using your device's share functionality. We do not receive, process, or store your exported data. Where and how you share or save the export is entirely your choice.

9. Children's Privacy

Inkluna is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your data is stored on your device, you have full control. You can delete individual dreams, reset your entire journal, or delete the app to remove all data. We do not retain any of your data on any server.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to: access all your data (it's on your device), delete your data at any time, export your dream journal, and disconnect sleep data access at any time. No account is required to use Inkluna.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns, contact us at privacy@ocoeestudios.com.
