Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

Your dreams are private. Inkluna is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not collect or transmit your personal data.

1. Information We Store

Data stored on your device only: Dream journal entries, voice notes, mood data, settings, themes, ink colors, fonts, passcode, and all personal preferences are stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export your journal.

Apple Health sleep data: If you choose to connect Apple Health, Inkluna reads sleep stage data (Awake, REM, Core, Deep) from Apple Health on your device. This data is read-only, stays on your device, and is never transmitted to any server. Inkluna does not write to Apple Health. Sleep stages are estimates provided by Apple Watch and are displayed as such.

Data we do NOT collect: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or any personal identifiers. We do not use tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or analytics frameworks that profile users. No data is transmitted off your device to our servers or to any third party.

2. Biometric Data

Inkluna supports Face ID and Touch ID for app lock authentication. Biometric data is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework on your device. We never access, store, or transmit biometric data. Authentication results are a simple yes/no — we never see your face or fingerprint.

3. How We Use Information

All data processing happens locally on your device. Inkluna uses your dream entries to display your journal, calculate patterns, populate your Dream Book, and show statistics. We do not use your dream content to train models, build user profiles, serve advertisements, or share with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

All journal data is stored locally on your device. Your passcode is stored in the iOS Keychain via secure encrypted storage. If you enable app lock, your data is protected by your chosen passcode and/or biometric authentication. We recommend keeping your device updated and using a strong device passcode for maximum security.

5. Purchases

Inkluna offers a one-time lifetime purchase ($19.99) to unlock premium features. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information, credit card numbers, or billing details. Inkluna only reads the local purchase entitlement to determine whether premium features are unlocked. Apple's privacy policy governs payment processing.

6. Third-Party Services

Apple StoreKit: Handles in-app purchase processing according to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple HealthKit: Provides read-only access to sleep data with your permission. Data stays on your device.

Apple LocalAuthentication: Handles Face ID / Touch ID for app lock. Biometric data stays on your device.

Inkluna does not integrate with any advertising, analytics, or data-processing services.

7. Data Export

You can export your dream journal at any time. Exported data is generated on your device and shared using the iOS share sheet. We do not receive, process, or store your exported data. Where and how you share or save the export is entirely your choice.

8. Children's Privacy

Inkluna is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your data is stored on your device, you have full control. You can delete individual dreams, reset your entire journal, or delete the app to remove all data. We do not retain any of your data on any server.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to: access all your data (it's on your device), delete your data at any time, export your dream journal, and disconnect Apple Health at any time. No account is required to use Inkluna.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns, contact us at privacy@ocoeestudios.com.
